# T2 learning design

TracksStore owns durable learning state and audit records. SQLite transactions serialize changes. Workers estimate effects from time-ordered nights. Missing data and failed guards abstain. Kinetics remain fixed through a global 28-day observation period and each field's minimum. Per-field periods are bounded, and tolerance/withdrawal wait for the greater of five half-lives and withdrawal duration. Steps change 1–3% of a field scale and respect locks.

The night adjustment is the existing max/min/OR ConfounderProfile monoid, extended with a set of user track identities and explanatory labels. Union preserves identity, associativity and commutativity. Preset behavior remains unchanged. Natural wake histories partition on the complete active identity set; independent user tracks cannot borrow warm-up nights.

Count, mean and M2 accumulators merge with the Welford formula. Effects are per selected system, shrink toward a preset prior or zero, and require matched clean comparisons, logging completeness, coverage, time ordering, identifiable co-exposures and permutation/negative-control checks with a family-wise threshold. Unsupported or inseparable effects stay absent. These are observational associations, not causal findings.
