# Tracks detection and experiments

ActivityStore owns review candidates and decisions. TracksStore owns track events,
experiment assignments, and explicit adherence. Detectors emit candidates only.
Missing-count prompts carry no observed time or quantity. Repeated derivations
must not refill a daily detection quota after dismissal or confirmation.

Alcohol evidence uses the first three hours of observed sleep, with HR above
and RMSSD below a personal early-sleep reference. Cannabis evidence uses a
resting HR rise of at least 15 bpm with measured low motion. Caffeine has no
detector. These signatures are nonspecific review aids, never diagnoses.
Custom detection requires a supported T2 effect and sufficient stable learning.
Selected affected systems constrain every detector.

Experiments randomize calendar dates in balanced blocks for 14, 21 or 28 days.
Assignments survive restarts and never imply that the wearer followed them.
Only explicit logs and absence confirmations establish adherence. The report
compares observed outcomes with uncertainty and reports missing coverage or
adherence. While an experiment is active, its eligible dates take precedence
for that track's estimation. No observational fallback fills an insufficient
experiment. Existing kinetics and global learning gates still apply.

All work proportional to stored history runs in a registered worker. Reads and
writes use immutable snapshots and reject stale results before commit. New
schema tables are additive, repaired on open, and included in backup, deletion
and wipe handling. Reminders pass through NotificationCenter and its preference
and fire-once gates.

## Implementation notes

`TracksStore.refreshDetections(date, windows)` takes observed windows (phase,
HR, RMSSD, personal baselines, motion, coverage), applies the detectors in
`lib/tracks/detection.dart`, honours each track's daily max (all non-superseded
candidates count, whatever the wearer decided) and reconciles through
`ActivityStore` (`ActivityKind.track`). Track candidates never merge across
tracks, ignore the sleep-overlap guard, and confirm writes one untimed-amount
`track_event`. A below-minimum day yields a prompt row (`details.prompt`): Yes
logs an untimed event, No records an absence. The window source (substrate to
windows) is the caller's; none is wired into the derive yet.

Experiments: tables `track_experiment` and `track_experiment_day` (schema 84).
The report reads stored nightly outcomes with SQL `json_extract`; a night
belongs to the calendar date of its onset. Reminders are descriptors emitted by
the cross-day pass through NotificationCenter on the route-keyed
`/tracks/experiment` prompt class. A running experiment replaces the track's
estimates with `experiment_learning` abstentions inside the learning worker.
